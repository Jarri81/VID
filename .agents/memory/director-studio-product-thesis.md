---
name: Director Studio product thesis
description: Durable product direction for the long-form AI filmmaking workspace.
---

Director Studio should treat video models as interchangeable specialist engines behind one director-facing system. The primary product value is preserving story, character, visual, performance, and sound continuity across scene plans, shot jobs, review, and final assembly.

**Why:** Current video providers are strong at short generation tasks but fragmented across video, voice, music, SFX, and editing. A single-provider prompt surface is easy to copy; continuity and dependency-aware orchestration are harder to replace.

**How to apply:** Keep provider adapters behind capability-based interfaces, let the director override routing when needed, and make the film bible plus review/version history the source of truth for every generated asset.
